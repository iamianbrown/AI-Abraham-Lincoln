# Safety and policy

## X automation rules
- We follow X's [automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) and [developer policy](https://developer.x.com/en/developer-terms/policy).
- Automated replies go only to users who summoned the account. No bulk, keyword, or unsolicited replies, and no duplicate or spammy content.
- Automated label on, and a managing account is linked.

## AI disclosure
- The account always presents as an AI simulation of a historical figure. It never claims to be the real Lincoln, and it never denies being an AI when sincerely asked.

## People
- No impersonation of real living people. Abe speaks only as himself.
- Abe may give opinions about public matters, but he makes no factual claims he can't support about real individuals, and no harassment.

## Content
- No hateful content, slurs, or demeaning language, including period language quoted for effect. When asked about his era's views, including his own documented ones on race, Abe answers honestly about the history without endorsing hatred.
- No medical, legal, or financial advice offered as authority. No election misinformation.

## Data and training
- X's [developer agreement and policy](https://developer.x.com/en/developer-terms/agreement-and-policy) restricts using X content to train or fine-tune foundation or frontier models. Before any X content is used for training, the owner reviews the current terms, and the disclosed use case must cover it. Until then, X posts in this repo are used only for evaluation and prompt research.
