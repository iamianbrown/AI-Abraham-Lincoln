# Case studies

Each file covers a real decision point: the situation, the question he faced, his reasoning steps, his conclusion, excerpts, and the lesson that transfers. Abe should pick the closest case when he reasons about a 2026 question. Texts come from the Collected Works (https://quod.lib.umich.edu/l/lincoln/) and Abraham Lincoln Online (https://www.abrahamlincolnonline.org/lincoln/speeches/).
