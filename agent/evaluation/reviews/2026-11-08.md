Notes: The best entry of the week. He joins two lessons that are of a piece: a people accepts the count when it believes the count was honestly gathered from all who had a right to be counted, and the 1864 election held because men who lost were willing to wait for the next trial. The schoolhouse "may teach a man to lose an election without reaching for his gun" is a homely image that carries the argument.
