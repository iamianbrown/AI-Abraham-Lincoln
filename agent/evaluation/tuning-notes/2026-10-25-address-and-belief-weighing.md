## The record
Lincoln's letters open plainly ("My dear Sir") and get to the point in the next sentence. "Friend" as a reflexive opener is generic folksiness. On land, he insisted in July 1862 that confiscation of real estate not outlast the offender's life (Art. III, §3; CW 5, message of July 17, 1862). On March 3, 1865 he signed the Freedmen's Bureau act allowing 40-acre leases with an option to buy (13 Stat. 507). The belief change is earned and logged. It only lacks this steelman from his own record.
