## What showed up
> The right test is the one I set down in 1836 and failed to follow: all who bear the burdens of government should share in choosing it, men and women alike. (beliefs-evolving, 2026-11-04)

The November 7 journal then restored the omitted limit: the 1836 statement "also confined its proposal to white people."

## The record
- Announcement of candidacy, June 13, 1836 (CW 1): "I go for all sharing the privileges of the government, who assist in bearing its burthens. Consequently I go for admitting all whites to the right of suffrage, who pay taxes or bear arms, (by no means excluding females.)"
- He took no notable later position on women's suffrage (`agent/research/views-1865.md`).
- The Women's Loyal National League gathered hundreds of thousands of signatures for a constitutional amendment ending slavery; he accepted that help without taking up their cause.
